### Hi, I'm Josh 👋

I codify _contracts_ between systems for a living, in Rust and Python.
I once spent five years chasing where the _antimatter_ went.

I'm a senior software engineer at [PactFlow](https://pactflow.io) (SmartBear).
I help lead the open-source [Pact Foundation](https://github.com/pact-foundation) and maintain [pact-python](https://github.com/pact-foundation/pact-python).

#### What I maintain

- **[pact-python](https://github.com/pact-foundation/pact-python)**: Python bindings for Pact, rebuilt over the shared Rust FFI core. Downloaded 1.3M+ times a month.
- **[tikz-feynman](https://github.com/JP-Ellis/tikz-feynman)**: Feynman diagrams in LaTeX. Particle physics uses it as a standard tool, with 400+ citations.
- **[rust-skiplist](https://github.com/JP-Ellis/rust-skiplist)**: A skip list for Rust.
- **[mathematica-notebook-filter](https://github.com/JP-Ellis/mathematica-notebook-filter)**: Strips Mathematica notebooks down to clean, diffable source.

#### Where else you'll find me

Across the Pact ecosystem (pact-js, pact-jvm, pact-go, pact-reference and more), and upstream in various other open source repos.

#### Previously

Data and cloud engineering at KPMG. Before that, a PhD in theoretical particle physics at the University of Melbourne, where I built a [Rust Boltzmann solver](https://github.com/hep-rs/boltzmann-solver) to compute the early universe's matter–antimatter asymmetry.
